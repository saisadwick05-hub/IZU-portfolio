01 / About

A little
about me.

IZU / Digital Designer 2026

I'm a digital designer who enjoys turning ideas into clean, expressive, and meaningful visual experiences. My work moves between web design, UI/UX, branding, motion, and visual communication.

I like design that feels intentional — simple enough to understand, but detailed enough to make people feel something.

Outside design, I'm drawn to poetry, literature, reading books, and exploring new ideas. I enjoy observing people, stories, visuals, and the small details that often become the starting point for my next idea.

I'm curious by nature and I like experimenting with different creative forms — from digital interfaces and visual identities to motion, writing, and experimental concepts. For me, design is not just about making things look good; it's about finding the right way to communicate an idea.
